# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or viewpoint.
We expect respectful, good-faith collaboration in repository discussions,
reviews, events, and other project spaces.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project conventions without gatekeeping.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and responsible disclosure.
- Accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behaviour.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised
attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, publishing private information, and
retaliation against a person who reports a concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@astralabs-ai.net](mailto:support@astralabs-ai.net). Do not use a public
issue when doing so could expose a target or reporter. Include relevant links,
dates, and context. Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to review
the report, consider conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately.

Possible actions include a private correction, warning, content removal,
temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. Immediate restrictions may be
used to protect participants while a report is reviewed. Knowingly false reports
or retaliation may also result in enforcement.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person is officially representing
the project. It complements, and does not replace, applicable platform terms or
law.
