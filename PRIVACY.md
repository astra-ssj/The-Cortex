# Community Privacy Notice

This notice covers participation in Astra GRC Community Edition repository
spaces. It is not a privacy policy for a hosted deployment of the software.
Operators of deployments are responsible for their own notices and obligations.

## Public contribution data

Issues, pull requests, commits, reviews, and discussions may publicly expose your
account name, profile, email embedded in Git metadata, contribution content, and
timestamps. Hosting providers process this information under their own terms.
Public contribution history may be copied into forks, archives, and releases and
cannot always be fully removed.

Do not submit personal data, credentials, confidential evidence, customer
records, or production logs. Use synthetic or redacted examples. Scenario
submissions should identify authors only as they wish to be publicly credited.

## Private reports

Security and conduct reports sent to the project contact may contain contact
details and report context. Maintainers use that information to investigate,
respond, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations, and limit access to people
who need it. Information may be retained with the project record when necessary
for security, enforcement, or legal purposes.

For a privacy question or a request concerning information controlled by the
project, email [support@astralabs-ai.net](mailto:support@astralabs-ai.net).
Identity verification or additional details may be required. Repository hosts
must be contacted directly for information they control.
